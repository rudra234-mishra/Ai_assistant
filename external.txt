Udayanath Autonomous College of Science and Technology
Udayanath Autonomous College of Science and Technology is one of the well-known higher educational institutions in Odisha. The college is located at Prachi Jnanapitha, Adaspur, in Cuttack district, Odisha, on the banks of the historic Prachi River. Its peaceful rural surroundings provide an ideal atmosphere for learning while remaining well connected to nearby towns and the city of Cuttack. The institution is affiliated with Utkal University and has been granted autonomous status, allowing it to design and implement its own curriculum while maintaining high academic standards. The college has grown into a respected center for higher education, serving students from different districts of Odisha.


The history of the college dates back to the early 1980s. The institution began as Prachi Mahavidyalaya and later evolved into Udayanath College with the generous support of Late Udayanath Sahoo, after whom the college is named. It formally developed into a full-fledged undergraduate institution in the early 1990s and received autonomous status from the University Grants Commission in 2009. Throughout its journey, the college has focused on providing quality education, especially to students from rural and semi-urban backgrounds, and has continuously expanded its academic programs and infrastructure.


The college is presently headed by Dr. Lulumina Dash, who serves as the Principal. Under her leadership, the institution emphasizes academic excellence, research, discipline, and the overall personality development of students. The college also has Prof. Arun Kumar Swain as its Director, who contributes to the institution's academic and administrative growth. Together, the leadership aims to create an environment that combines traditional values with modern education and technology.


Udayanath Autonomous College offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across multiple disciplines. Students can pursue degrees in Arts, Science, and Commerce, along with several professional and self-financing courses. Popular undergraduate programs include Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA), Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA), B.Sc. in Computer Science, and B.Sc. in Information Technology Management (ITM). At the postgraduate level, the college offers programs such as M.Sc. in Computer Science and other master's degree courses in various disciplines. These programs are designed to provide students with both theoretical knowledge and practical skills required for higher education and employment.


The college has developed modern infrastructure to support quality education. It houses well-equipped laboratories, smart classrooms, a central library with thousands of books and journals, computer laboratories with internet connectivity, separate hostels for boys and girls, guest house facilities, seminar halls, and research laboratories. The campus also includes an international-standard swimming pool, a gymnasium, and various facilities that encourage students to participate in extracurricular activities alongside academics.


One of the major attractions of the institution is its sports infrastructure. The college has a spacious stadium where students regularly participate in athletics, football, cricket, volleyball, and other sporting events. Sports competitions, annual athletic meets, and cultural festivals are organized throughout the year to encourage teamwork, leadership, and physical fitness. The campus provides a healthy balance between academics and extracurricular activities, allowing students to develop their overall personality.


The learning environment at Udayanath Autonomous College is widely appreciated for its discipline and student-friendly atmosphere. Located away from the noise and congestion of urban areas, the campus offers a calm and peaceful setting that helps students focus on their studies. Faculty members are experienced and supportive, encouraging students to participate in seminars, workshops, research projects, and community outreach activities. The institution also promotes innovation, leadership, ethical values, and social responsibility among its students.


Placement support is another important aspect of the college. A dedicated Training and Placement Cell organizes career guidance programs, aptitude training, soft-skills development, resume-building workshops, mock interviews, and campus recruitment drives. Students are encouraged to participate in internships, industrial visits, and skill development programs that improve their employability. While placement opportunities vary depending on the course and market conditions, the institution works continuously to strengthen its industry connections and prepare students for careers in both the public and private sectors.


Apart from academics, the college actively promotes cultural, literary, and social activities. Students participate in NSS, NCC, debates, quizzes, science exhibitions, cultural festivals, environmental awareness campaigns, and community service initiatives. These activities help students build confidence, communication skills, leadership qualities, and a sense of responsibility toward society.


In conclusion, Udayanath Autonomous College of Science and Technology has established itself as a reputed institution of higher education in Odisha through its commitment to academic excellence, modern infrastructure, experienced faculty, and holistic student development. With a wide variety of academic programs, excellent campus facilities, a peaceful environment, and continuous efforts toward skill development and placements, the college continues to shape the future of thousands of students and contribute significantly to the educational development of the region.
